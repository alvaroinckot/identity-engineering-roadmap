# European Union: identity regulation

**TL;DR:** The EU regulates identity systems through several acts. Regulations apply in their
entirety across the EU; directives set goals that member states transpose into national law.
GDPR governs identity data as personal data; eIDAS 2.0 requires each member state to provide a
Digital Identity Wallet and requires listed relying parties to accept it when its triggers are
met; NIS2 requires access control policies and, where appropriate, multi-factor or continuous
authentication from critical-sector entities, and DORA requires strong authentication and
monitoring from financial entities; PSD2 and its technical standards define strong customer
authentication for payments; the AI Act requires logging and human oversight for AI systems
classified as high-risk under Art. 6 and Annex III. Recurring controls: strong authentication,
need-based access, logging, timed breach reporting, human oversight.

Each act binds a different population and reaches identity systems through a different article:
GDPR (Art. 3, 4, 5, 25, 32, 33, 22) binds controllers and processors established in the EU and
treats identifiers as personal data when they relate to an identified or identifiable natural
person — see [GDPR: identity data as personal data](gdpr-identity-data.md); eIDAS 2.0 (Art. 5a,
5b, 5f) binds member states, registered relying parties and very large online platforms around
the wallet — see [eIDAS 2.0 & the EU Digital Identity Wallet](eidas-2-eudi-wallet.md); NIS2
(Art. 21, 23) binds essential and important entities and DORA (Art. 9, 10) binds financial
entities — see [NIS2 & DORA: access control and MFA as legal duties](nis2-dora.md); PSD2 (Art.
4(30), 97, 98) and Delegated Regulation 2018/389 bind payment service providers — see [PSD2 &
strong customer authentication](psd2-strong-customer-authentication.md); the AI Act (Art. 12,
14, 26, Annex III) binds providers and deployers of high-risk AI systems — see [EU AI Act:
logging & human oversight](eu-ai-act.md).

_Not legal advice. Obligations and dates are quoted as published on 2026-09-17; check the
official text before relying on them._
