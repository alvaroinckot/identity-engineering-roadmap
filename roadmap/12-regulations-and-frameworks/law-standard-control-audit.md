# Law, standard, control & audit

**TL;DR:** EU legal acts differ in whether they bind and whom they cover: a regulation is "a
binding legislative act" applied in full in every member state, while a directive sets a goal
that each member state reaches through its own national law. ISO says its standards are
internationally agreed by experts and describe a way of doing something; a standard is not
itself legislation. A control is one prescribed safeguard: NIST SP 800-53 defines a security
control as the "safeguards or countermeasures prescribed for an information system or an
organization" to protect confidentiality, integrity and availability. Certification is written
assurance from an independent body that requirements are met, and ISO itself certifies nobody.
An attestation report, such as SOC 2, is an auditor's examination report on an organization's
controls.

These terms describe different layers between an obligation and a running system. The EU page
distinguishes binding regulations, directives and decisions from non-binding recommendations
and opinions; ISO says certification is a legal or contractual requirement in some industries,
and a standard is not itself legislation; a control is the unit an identity system is built and
inspected against, in SP 800-53's glossary sense. Evidence then comes in two shapes:
certification, which ISO defines as "the provision by an independent body of written assurance
(a certificate)" and which is issued by external certification bodies that may themselves be
accredited, and attestation, a CPA's examination report on a service organization's description
of its system and its controls, such as [SOC 2 & the Trust Services
Criteria](soc-2-trust-services-criteria.md). For agents, identify the applicable legal
obligations, the standards, the controls and the evidence separately; the build-versus-buy side
of that chain is covered in [Choosing an IdP: build, buy,
certify](../13-standards-watch/choosing-an-idp.md).

_Not legal advice. Obligations and dates are quoted as published on 2026-09-17; check the
official text before relying on them._
