## What AI agents change

The attacker no longer needs a person to click: prompt injection turns an agent's own
permissions into the exploit. Least privilege and explicit delegation chains
(→ [07](../07-workload-and-agent-identity/README.md), [08](../08-delegation-and-impersonation/README.md)) are
the mitigations.
